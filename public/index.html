            Intraday

            Open and close positions in the same trading day.

            Swing

            Hold positions overnight; use daily trend and exit signals.

            Combined allocations may be at most 100%. Remaining funds stay unallocated.

            Existing holdings

            Automatic management is separate from buying new stocks.

            Separate symbols with commas. Unselected holdings are visible and analysed, but are not automatically sold. Paper mode simulates sales.

            Unattended delivery sales require suitable demat authorisation. The engine checks this before placing real delivery orders.

            Pause trading and close managed positions before changing allocations.

            Zerodha app URLs

            These addresses follow the URL you used to open this dashboard.

            Open the dashboard through your Cloudflare Tunnel address to obtain public URLs. Copy Redirect URL and Postback URL into your Kite app. A new Quick Tunnel address requires updating both URLs.

            Only KITE_API_KEY, KITE_API_SECRET and KITE_USER_ID belong in .env. Configure the remaining preferences below.
